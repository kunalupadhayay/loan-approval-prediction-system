    How the model reads an application

    Credit history first

    Across thousands of training examples, a clean repayment history is the strongest single predictor of approval — more than income alone.

    02

    Income relative to the ask

    The model weighs the requested loan amount and term against combined household income, not income in isolation.

    03

    Everything else, in moderation

    Existing loans, education, and property area shift the odds slightly — enough to matter in borderline cases, not enough to override the fundamentals.
